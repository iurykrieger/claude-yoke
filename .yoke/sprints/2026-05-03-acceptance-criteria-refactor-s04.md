## Sprint objective

Update Sr QA and Sr Staff persona prompts so they read the new
`acceptance-criteria` artifact shape, select pool sensors per
Acceptance Criterion at runtime, and record their selection rationale
in their per-cycle slice file under `.yoke/runtime/cycles/<N>/`. Update
`lib/runtime/cycle.sh` and `lib/runtime/council.sh` to resolve
`.yoke/acceptance-criteria/<slug>.md` (via
`wm_acceptance_criteria_path`) and parse the `US-### / AC-<US>-<n>`
hierarchy through a new helper `lib/runtime/criteria-parse.sh`. Update
the Phase B contradiction-detection arbiter (`agents/council-arbiter.md`)
to classify AC-level disagreements between Sr QA and Sr Staff as
direct contradictions while sensor-selection disagreements remain
importance disagreements. The Phase A/B/C cycle machinery (sync
barrier, deterministic merge, JSON verdict schema) is not touched —
only the artifact-shape inputs and the persona prompts.
