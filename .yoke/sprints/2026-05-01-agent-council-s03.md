## Sprint objective

Make each persona's behavior match its stated objective and prove
that no persona can be reproduced by a v2.x agent with a prompt
tweak alone (the persona-overlap risk in the PRD). Sr Eng's prompt
focuses on shipping working code and writing happy-path unit tests;
Sr QA's Phase A includes writing acceptance-contract-anchored tests
under `tests/acceptance/<contract-slug>/` plus judging sensor
verdicts; Sr Staff invokes the configured `review-skill` (default
`/review`), consults canonical memory for architectural pattern
alignment, and applies a long-term-sustainability lens. At sprint
end, a council cycle on a real Yoke task surfaces at least one
finding from each persona's distinct lens that the v2.x runtime
provably could not have produced.
