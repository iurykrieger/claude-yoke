### Inferential sensors — calibration metadata

No inferential sensor is declared in this contract. The verdict envelope for inferential sensors is exercised **indirectly** via the scenarios that use `dispatch-by-type.test.sh` (which spawns a stub agent to validate dispatch) and via the `verdict-parser-rejects-*` sensors (which exercise the parser of the extended envelope). No real LLM-as-judge gates ratification — every decision is deterministic.

Real inferential sensors (e.g. `semantic-judge` reused) only re-enter contracts post-realignment, when the new shape lets them be declared cleanly per criterion.

`hooks/verify-acceptance.sh` parses the registry and the per-sensor files
to execute the contract.
