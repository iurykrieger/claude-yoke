## Amendment history

- **2026-04-27 (cycle 1, Trigger-3 mini-cycle, option ζ).** Fixed the
  `vibeflow-inventory-completeness` sensor command. Original used `diff`
  on shape-mismatched LHS (`path:line:content` from `grep | sort`) vs
  RHS (`path:line` from `awk | grep -oE | sort -u`); the diff
  byte-compared full lines and flagged every match as different even
  on a correct inventory — i.e., the sensor could never pass. The
  amendment normalizes both sides to token form (`path:line`) and uses
  `comm -23 | wc -l` to test set-difference cardinality (expect 0).
  Sensor intent (every live grep match has a corresponding inventory
  entry in the task file) is preserved; mechanism corrected. No other
  criterion or sensor is affected. Surfaced as artifact contradiction
  #2 in cycle 1's progress.md notes by the Generator.
